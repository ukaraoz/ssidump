East River · SSI ess-dive datasets export

Data points per site, depth, year and variable

Each site and depth has one row per year with data. Each cell counts the measurements in essdive_all.tsv.gz for one variable in that year. Water level and temperature datasets ingested from ESS-DIVE. Click a site row to collapse or expand its years. The all-years version is ESS-DIVE datasets coverage.

Source: essdive_all.tsv.gz, exported from the SSI database with ssiexport.py export datastreams --family ess_dive. Hover or tap a cell for the first and last sample date in that year. Years with no data at a site are not shown. Depths are in meters as recorded by the datastream.
