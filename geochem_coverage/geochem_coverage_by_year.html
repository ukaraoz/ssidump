East River · SSI geochem export

Data points per site, depth, year and analyte

Each site and depth has one row per year with data. Each cell counts the measurements in geochemdata_all.tsv for one analyte in that year. Rows without a depth are surface water or sites where the export records no depth. Click a site row to collapse or expand its years. The all-years version is Geochem coverage.

Source: geochemdata_all.tsv, exported from the SSI database with ssiexport.py export datastreams --family geochem. Hover or tap a cell for the first and last sample date in that year. Years with no data at a site are not shown. Depths are in meters as recorded by the datastream.
