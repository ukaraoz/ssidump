East River · SSI hillslope loggers m1–m4 export

Data points per site, depth, year and variable

Each site and depth has one row per year with data. Each cell counts the measurements in mloggers_all.tsv.gz for one variable in that year. M1–M4 are the CR1000 loggers (tables m1_10, m2_20, m3_30, m4_40) at the PLM hillslope boreholes and wells, ER-PLM1–4 and ER-PLM6. The export records no sensor depths. Click a site row to collapse or expand its years. The all-years version is Hillslope loggers M1–M4 coverage.

Source: mloggers_all.tsv.gz, exported from the SSI database with ssiexport.py export datastreams --pattern "^m[1-4]_". Hover or tap a cell for the first and last sample date in that year. Years with no data at a site are not shown. Depths are in meters as recorded by the datastream.
