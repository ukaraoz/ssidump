East River · SSI sensor towers export

Data points per site, depth and variable

Each cell counts the measurements in towers_all.tsv.gz for one variable at one site and sampling depth. The Pumphouse (ER-PHS1–4), Snodgrass (SG-EHS5–8) and Trail Creek (TR-EHS1–2) towers. Their Decagon soil sensors are on the Decagon page. For a year-by-year breakdown see Sensor towers by year.

Source: towers_all.tsv.gz, exported from the SSI database with ssiexport.py export datastreams --pattern "^feeder_(er|sg|tr)_". Hover or tap a cell for its date range. Depths are in meters as recorded by the datastream.
