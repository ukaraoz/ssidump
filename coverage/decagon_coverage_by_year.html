East River · SSI decagon sensors export

Data points per site, depth, year and variable

Each site and depth has one row per year with data. Each cell counts the measurements in decagon_all.tsv.gz for one variable in that year. Several sensors at one station can report the same variable (for example soil temperature from MPS-6, 5TE and ECT probes on different ports); their counts add up in one cell. The export records no sensor depths. The four Pumphouse and four Snodgrass towers also log weather and instrument readings. Click a site row to collapse or expand its years. The all-years version is Decagon sensors coverage.

Source: decagon_all.tsv.gz, exported from the SSI database with ssiexport.py export datastreams --family decagon. Hover or tap a cell for the first and last sample date in that year. Years with no data at a site are not shown. Depths are in meters as recorded by the datastream.
