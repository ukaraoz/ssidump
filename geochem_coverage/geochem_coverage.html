East River · SSI geochem export

Data points per site, depth and analyte

Each cell counts the measurements in geochemdata_all.tsv for one analyte at one site and sampling depth. Rows without a depth are surface water or sites where the export records no depth. For a year-by-year breakdown see Geochem by year.

Source: geochemdata_all.tsv, exported from the SSI database with ssiexport.py export datastreams --family geochem. Hover or tap a cell for its date range. Depths are in meters as recorded by the datastream.
