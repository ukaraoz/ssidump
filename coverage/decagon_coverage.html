East River · SSI decagon sensors export

Data points per site, depth and variable

Each cell counts the measurements in decagon_all.tsv.gz for one variable at one site and sampling depth. Several sensors at one station can report the same variable (for example soil temperature from MPS-6, 5TE and ECT probes on different ports); their counts add up in one cell. The export records no sensor depths. The four Pumphouse and four Snodgrass towers also log weather and instrument readings. For a year-by-year breakdown see Decagon sensors by year.

Source: decagon_all.tsv.gz, exported from the SSI database with ssiexport.py export datastreams --family decagon. Hover or tap a cell for its date range. Depths are in meters as recorded by the datastream.
