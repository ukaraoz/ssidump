East River · SSI snotel sites export

Data points per site, depth, year and variable

Each site and depth has one row per year with data. Each cell counts the measurements in snotel_all.tsv.gz for one variable in that year. NRCS SNOTEL stations ingested into the SSI database. Records start in 1997. Click a site row to collapse or expand its years. The all-years version is SNOTEL sites coverage.

Source: snotel_all.tsv.gz, exported from the SSI database with ssiexport.py export datastreams --family snotel. Hover or tap a cell for the first and last sample date in that year. Years with no data at a site are not shown. Depths are in meters as recorded by the datastream.
