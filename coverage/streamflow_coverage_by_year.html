East River · SSI streamflow gauges export

Data points per site, depth, year and variable

Each site and depth has one row per year with data. Each cell counts the measurements in streamflow_all.tsv for one variable in that year. Click a site row to collapse or expand its years. The all-years version is Streamflow gauges coverage.

Source: streamflow_all.tsv, exported from the SSI database with ssiexport.py export datastreams --family streamflow. Hover or tap a cell for the first and last sample date in that year. Years with no data at a site are not shown. Depths are in meters as recorded by the datastream.
