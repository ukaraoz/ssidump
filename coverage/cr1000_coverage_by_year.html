East River · SSI cr1000 weather stations export

Data points per site, depth, year and variable

Each site and depth has one row per year with data. Each cell counts the measurements in cr1000_all.tsv.gz for one variable in that year. Campbell CR1000 loggers at six weather stations (Almont, Billy Barr, Judd Falls, Kettle Ponds, Mexican Cut, Snodgrass Mountain), the YSI water-quality sonde at Pumphouse ISCO (ER-PHF0) and the PH_SOLINST barometric logger (ER-PHF2). M1–M4 have their own page. Click a site row to collapse or expand its years. The all-years version is CR1000 weather stations coverage.

Source: cr1000_all.tsv.gz, exported from the SSI database with ssiexport.py export datastreams --pattern "^(am|bb|jf|kp|sm|mc|cb)_". Hover or tap a cell for the first and last sample date in that year. Years with no data at a site are not shown. Depths are in meters as recorded by the datastream.
