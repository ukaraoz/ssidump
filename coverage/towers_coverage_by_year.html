East River · SSI sensor towers export

Data points per site, depth, year and variable

Each site and depth has one row per year with data. Each cell counts the measurements in towers_all.tsv.gz for one variable in that year. The Pumphouse (ER-PHS1–4), Snodgrass (SG-EHS5–8) and Trail Creek (TR-EHS1–2) towers. Their Decagon soil sensors are on the Decagon page. Click a site row to collapse or expand its years. The all-years version is Sensor towers coverage.

Source: towers_all.tsv.gz, exported from the SSI database with ssiexport.py export datastreams --pattern "^feeder_(er|sg|tr)_". Hover or tap a cell for the first and last sample date in that year. Years with no data at a site are not shown. Depths are in meters as recorded by the datastream.
