East River · SSI usgs nwis gauges export

Data points per site, depth, year and variable

Each site and depth has one row per year with data. Each cell counts the measurements in nwis_all.tsv.gz for one variable in that year. USGS National Water Information System gauges ingested into the SSI database. Click a site row to collapse or expand its years. The all-years version is USGS NWIS gauges coverage.

Source: nwis_all.tsv.gz, exported from the SSI database with ssiexport.py export datastreams --family nwis. Hover or tap a cell for the first and last sample date in that year. Years with no data at a site are not shown. Depths are in meters as recorded by the datastream.
