East River · SSI cr1000 weather stations export

Data points per site, depth and variable

Each cell counts the measurements in cr1000_all.tsv.gz for one variable at one site and sampling depth. Campbell CR1000 loggers at six weather stations (Almont, Billy Barr, Judd Falls, Kettle Ponds, Mexican Cut, Snodgrass Mountain), the YSI water-quality sonde at Pumphouse ISCO (ER-PHF0) and the PH_SOLINST barometric logger (ER-PHF2). M1–M4 have their own page. For a year-by-year breakdown see CR1000 weather stations by year.

Source: cr1000_all.tsv.gz, exported from the SSI database with ssiexport.py export datastreams --pattern "^(am|bb|jf|kp|sm|mc|cb)_". Hover or tap a cell for its date range. Depths are in meters as recorded by the datastream.
